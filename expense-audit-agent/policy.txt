COMPANY EXPENSE POLICY
Effective: January 1, 2024
Last Updated: October 15, 2024

1. GENERAL RULES

All expenses must have a clear business purpose. Receipts are required for
any item over $25. Reports must be submitted within 30 days of the expense.
Expenses older than 90 days will not be reimbursed.

2. TRAVEL

Flights
  - Domestic and flights under 6 hours: Economy class only.
  - Flights 6 hours or longer: Business class permitted without additional
    approval.
  - First-class travel is not reimbursable under any circumstances.

Hotels
  - Domestic: Up to $250 per night.
  - International: Up to $350 per night.
  - Amounts above these limits require VP approval before booking.

Ground Transportation
  - Standard rideshare services (UberX, Lyft standard) are reimbursable.
  - Premium rideshare (Uber Black, Lyft Lux) requires written justification.
  - Mileage for personal vehicle: $0.67 per mile.
  - Parking and tolls are reimbursable with receipts.

3. MEALS

Daily Limits
  - Domestic travel: $75 per day.
  - International travel: $100 per day.
  - Daily limits apply to the total of all meals in a calendar day.

Client Meals
  - Up to $100 per person.
  - Names and company affiliations of all attendees must be documented.

Team Meals
  - Manager approval required for groups of 4 or more.
  - Per-person cost should be reasonable and within the daily limit.

Alcohol
  - Reimbursable only when clients are present.
  - Not reimbursable for solo meals or internal-only meals.

Per Diem
  - Employees may choose per diem OR actual meal reimbursement, not both.
  - Per diem rates: $75/day domestic, $100/day international.
  - Per diem does not require receipts.

4. ENTERTAINMENT AND GIFTS

Client Entertainment
  - Up to $150 per person with prior manager approval.
  - Business purpose and attendee names must be documented.

Gifts
  - Up to $100 per recipient per calendar year.
  - Gifts to government employees are prohibited.

Events and Tickets
  - Requires VP approval regardless of amount.

5. OFFICE SUPPLIES AND SUBSCRIPTIONS

  - Office supplies under $50: No approval needed.
  - Office supplies $50-$200: Manager approval required.
  - Software subscriptions: Must be on the company-approved software list
    or have written IT department approval.
  - Personal equipment (bags, accessories, etc.): Not reimbursable unless
    specifically required for business travel and pre-approved.

6. APPROVAL THRESHOLDS

  - Under $500 total: Self-approved (receipts still required over $25).
  - $500 to $2,000 total: Manager approval required.
  - Over $2,000 total: VP approval required.

7. PROHIBITED EXPENSES

  - Personal expenses of any kind.
  - First-class air travel.
  - Alcohol without clients present.
  - Expenses submitted more than 90 days after the date incurred.
  - Cash advances without prior written approval.
  - Spouse or companion travel expenses.
